Hello! I'm Zeyu "Claude" Gao, a passionate lover of Media & Communication. Currently, I am pursing a PhD in Communication at [University of Connecticut](https://communication.uconn.edu/). Before that, I served as a lecturer at Department of Journalism, [Yantai Institute of Technology](https://www.yitsd.edu.cn/). I earned my MSc from [University of Glasgow](https://www.gla.ac.uk/research/az/gumg/) and my BA from [Yantai University](https://www.ytu.edu.cn/).

My research interests include Human-Machine Communication (HMC) and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). I enjoy using quantitative and computational methods (experiment, survey, SEM, NLP, big data, network analysis, etc.). If you have any communication or cooperation intentions, please feel free to contact me via e-mail (gaozeyu01@sina.com).
